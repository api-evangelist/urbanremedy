# Urban Remedy

> Urban Remedy is a certified B Corp organic food company that makes fresh, organic, plant-based, ready-to-eat meals, cold pressed juices, juice cleanses, and snacks, delivered nationwide and sold through its own retail stores and kiosks. Founded by Neka Pasquale on the belief that food is healing.
